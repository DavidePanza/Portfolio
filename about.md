<p>I'm a data scientist with a computational neuroscience background and 3+ years of hands-on experience in machine learning, data analysis, and AI. </p>

<p>I started in neuroscience research, analyzing how people recognize objects using neural networks and EEG data. After completing an intensive program at Data Science Retreat, I made the jump to applied data science. My work now focuses on machine learning and data systems, like building recommendation engines, processing large datasets, and getting models to actually work in production. I'm working with language models and computer vision, but I'm just as interested in the data engineering side and ensuring everything runs smoothly behind the scenes.</p>
